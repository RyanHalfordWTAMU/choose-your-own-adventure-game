# Alderaan Adventure

A text-based choose-your-own-adventure game: the player creates a character, then travels from the crossroads to hunt in the forest, trade at the inn, or face the dragon.

## Language

### Places

**Crossroads**:
The starting point of every journey, where the player chooses north, south or west.
_Avoid_: Path prompt, start, mountains

**Forest**:
The woods south of the crossroads, where the player hunts.
_Avoid_: Woods, south path

**Inn**:
The building west of the crossroads where the Innkeeper and the Merchant trade.
_Avoid_: Tavern, pub, shop

### Characters

**Player**:
The character the person at the keyboard creates and controls.
_Avoid_: Hero, user

**Dragon**:
The hostile creature met on the north path; the game's only combat opponent.
_Avoid_: Monster, enemy

**Merchant**:
A trader at the Inn who sells magical items for gold.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, vendor, store

**Innkeeper**:
The keeper of the Inn, who buys Meat and Hides and sells food and drink.
_Avoid_: Barkeep, bartender, tavern keeper

### Hunting

**Hunt**:
One attempt in the Forest to track and catch an animal for its Meat and Hide.
_Avoid_: Search, forage

**Meat**:
A good taken from a hunted animal, which the player can sell or eat.
_Avoid_: Food, rations

**Hide**:
An animal skin taken from a hunted Deer or Boar, which the player can only sell.
_Avoid_: Pelt, leather, fur

**Hunger**:
How in need of food the player is, from 0 (fed) to 10 (Starving).
_Avoid_: Food meter, appetite

**Thirst**:
How in need of drink the player is, from 0 (quenched) to 10 (Parched).
_Avoid_: Water meter, hydration

### Trade

**Gold**:
The player's money, earned by selling Meat and Hides and spent at the Inn.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, currency

**Magical Armor**:
An item sold by the merchant that grants the player bonus health points.
_Avoid_: Shield, protection

**Enchantment**:
A magical upgrade the merchant applies to the player's existing weapon, raising its maximum damage. A weapon that has one is an **Enchanted Weapon**.
_Avoid_: Magic weapon, new weapon, upgrade
